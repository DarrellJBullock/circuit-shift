## Description (≤ 4000)
Circuit Shift is a calm, clever puzzle game about routing power. Tap tiles to rotate them, connect the amber source to every receiver, and watch the current flow through your circuit.

THREE CHAPTERS, 60 PUZZLES
• Ignition: learn corners, junctions and crossings.
• Grid Lock: juggle several power sources and branching paths.
• Spectrum: route cyan and violet signals that must never touch.

EVERY PUZZLE IS FAIR
Every puzzle's shortest solution is verified, so the star rating always measures your efficiency against a known best. Undo, reset and a few free hints per level keep you moving.

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
The Daily Challenge is the same puzzle for everyone, with a fresh one each day and the past week ready to replay.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Works completely offline, with no account required.
• No energy systems, no timers to wait out, and no forced ads.
• Optional: watch a short video for an extra hint, or remove ads with a one-time purchase.
• Built for everyone: VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, haptics and sound you can switch off, and shapes as well as colours to tell signals apart.

Your progress stays on your device.
