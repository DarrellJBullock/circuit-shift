# Circuit Shift Support

**Contact:** support@evercrest-interactive.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I play?**
Tap a tile to rotate it clockwise. Connect the amber source to every receiver. When every receiver is
lit and no wire is left dangling, the circuit is complete.

**What do the stars mean?**
Every puzzle's shortest solution is known. Three stars: at most about 11% more moves than the
minimum (90% efficiency). Two stars: at most about 54% more (65% efficiency). Any finish earns at least
one star. Using a hint caps that level at two stars.

**Why won't two colours connect?**
In the Spectrum chapter, cyan and violet signals must never share a network. If they meet, the circuit
shorts, which is shown in red with a broken wire.

**I lost my progress.**
Progress is stored on your device only. It is removed if the app is deleted, and it does not transfer
between devices.

**How do I restore a purchase?**
Shop → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

**How do I change ad consent?**
Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where consent applies).

**Accessibility**
Circuit Shift supports VoiceOver (double-tap a tile to rotate it), Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, and
separate switches for sound and haptics. Signals use shapes as well as colours.
