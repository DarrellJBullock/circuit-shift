# App Review information: DRAFT

## Notes for the reviewer
Circuit Shift is an offline puzzle game. No account or sign-in is required, so no demo account is needed.

- **How to play:** Tap tiles to rotate them and connect the amber source to every receiver. Levels
  1-1 to 1-3 are a short tutorial (tap "Skip" to bypass it).
- **To reach later content quickly:** chapters unlock after 15 levels of the previous chapter. The
  Daily Challenge (Home → Daily Challenge) is always available and shows a full-size board.
- **In-app purchases** (Shop): in 1.0, only "Remove Ads" (non-consumable; hints never require a
  video). "Premium Chapters" and "Founder Bundle" are hidden by the app until a premium chapter ships,
  so **do not submit those two IAPs with 1.0**. Restore purchases is in the Shop.
- **Ads:** rewarded video only, shown only when the player taps "Hint · Ad" after using the three
  free hints in a level. There are no banners, interstitials or app-open ads. Ads are
  non-personalized; the app does not request App Tracking Transparency.
  Until the app is published and approved in AdMob, Google may have no ad to serve. "Hint · Ad" then
  shows "No ad is available right now. Try again later." and grants no hint. This is expected:
  the rest of the game, including the three free hints per level, is unaffected.
- **Privacy:** game progress is stored locally on the device. Privacy policy: https://evercrest-interactive.com/apps/circuit-shift/privacy.html (also linked from Settings).
